Help & Account Security

Only your username and profile photo can be updated directly. Security-sensitive fields require administrator verification.

How do I change my email or birth date?

For security reasons these fields are locked — they can only be changed by the System Administrator (contact details below). Send your request from your registered email address and include your username plus the new value (birth date as YYYY-MM-DD).

How do I change my username?

Click Edit username / password, type your new username (1–30 characters), and save.

How do I change my profile picture?

Click the camera button on your avatar, then choose a PNG, JPG, or WebP image (up to 2 MB).

How do I change my password?

Click Edit username / password and complete the Change password form using your current password.

Is the money real?

No. LadaStocks is a paper-trading simulator — every account starts with $100,000.00 in simulated funds so you can practice trading risk-free.
