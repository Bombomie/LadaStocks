Help & FAQ

How do I change my username?

Click Edit profile, type your new username and save. Usernames must be 1–30 characters.

How do I change my email or birth date?

For security reasons these fields are locked — they can only be changed by the System Administrator (contact details below). Send your request from your registered email address and include your username plus the new value (birth date as YYYY-MM-DD). The administrator will confirm once your profile has been updated.

How do I change my profile picture?

Click the camera button on your avatar, then choose a PNG, JPG or WebP image (up to 2 MB). The new picture is applied immediately here and in the navbar.

How do I change my password?

Use the Change password module on this page. You need your current password. If you forgot it, contact the administrator to have it reset.

Is the money real?

No. Lada Stocks is a paper-trading simulator — every account starts with a wallet of 100,000.00 in simulated funds, so you can practice trading with zero real-world risk.
